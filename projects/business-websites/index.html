Business Websites: WordPress Delivery, Performance & SEO

Business websites I built and led since 2018: iLab, SpeedLogi, SpeedPoint, The MK Solution and Sagal Energy, with my WordPress, performance and SEO approach.

Overview

Since 2018 I have built and managed business websites: as Project Manager & Senior WordPress Developer at The MK Solution (2018–2024), as a freelance WordPress developer on Upwork (2020–2023), and on company websites at iLab Information Technologies / SpeedLogi in Al Khobar (2025–2026). The work ranges from corporate sites to service pages for logistics and energy businesses.

Selected websites

  • iLab Information Technologies: corporate website work focused on credibility, site speed, SEO structure and clear service presentation.
  • SpeedLogi: website improvements for brand presence, performance and clearer discovery of logistics services.
  • SpeedPoint: an operational platform addition for the SpeedLogi ecosystem, with delivery-oriented UX and structured workflows.
  • The MK Solution: corporate website delivery for a digital services firm.
  • Sagal Energy Associates: a business website with a clean user experience and organised content structure.

Leading website delivery at The MK Solution

  • Managed a 10-person cross-functional team on high-visibility client projects.
  • Led complex WordPress builds using Elementor, from structure to launch.
  • Implemented SEO, security and responsive design best practices across projects.
  • Mentored junior developers and improved the team’s delivery standards.

Freelance WordPress work

  • Built custom WordPress themes and designs with Elementor for clients on Upwork.
  • Maintained multiple live websites with updates and troubleshooting.
  • Extended sites with custom HTML, CSS, PHP and JavaScript where page builders were not enough.
  • Delivered on tight timelines while keeping quality consistent.

What I focus on

  • Clear service presentation: visitors should understand what the business offers and how to get in touch within seconds.
  • Performance: optimised images, lean pages and caching so sites load quickly on mobile networks.
  • Search structure: unique titles and descriptions, sensible headings, clean URLs and a sitemap.
  • Responsive design: layouts, navigation and forms that work on phones first.
  • Security and maintainability: updates, sensible plugins and clear ownership after launch.

How I run a website project

  • Discovery: agree on the audience, the main action each page should drive, and the scope.
  • Structure and content: plan pages and navigation, and collect the real content before design.
  • Build: implement in WordPress/Elementor or custom code, mobile first.
  • Test: check navigation, forms, page titles, images and speed on real devices.
  • Launch and handover: confirm contact routes, search basics and who maintains the site afterwards.

Technology

  • WordPress, Elementor and custom themes
  • HTML, CSS, JavaScript and PHP
  • On-page SEO, sitemaps and search console setup
  • Responsive layouts and performance optimisation
