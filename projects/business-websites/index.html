Business Websites: Delivery, Performance & SEO

An overview of my WordPress and business website work, including iLab, SpeedLogi, and team leadership at The MK Solution.

Work and responsibilities

My portfolio includes corporate website work for iLab and improvements to SpeedLogi’s website. At The MK Solution, I managed a 10-person cross-functional team and led WordPress builds using Elementor.

Areas of focus

The work focused on service presentation, responsive design, site performance, SEO structure, and maintainability. These responsibilities connected business content with implementation and delivery standards.

How I approach a website project

I start with the audience and the action the page should support, agree on scope and content, then build and test the website. Before handover, I review mobile navigation, forms, page titles, image delivery, and ownership of ongoing maintenance.
