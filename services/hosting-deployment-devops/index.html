Hosting, Deployment & DevOps

Hosting, deployment and DevOps services: cPanel and Linux VPS setup, domains, SSL, business email, safe deployments, scheduled jobs, backups and monitoring.

Good software still fails on poor hosting: expired SSL, missing backups, risky manual deployments or jobs that run twice. I set up and run the environment your website or application lives on, from shared cPanel hosting to Linux VPS servers, with safe deployments, scheduled jobs, backups and monitoring.

For DMS I managed VPS deployments, scheduled jobs and a self-hosted real-time calling server, and this website runs on cPanel with automated packaging, private configuration outside the web root and tested releases.

Hosting, Deployment & DevOps: frequently asked questions

Can you move my site to new hosting?

Yes, with a backup first and minimal downtime, including email and DNS changes.

Shared hosting or VPS?

Shared hosting suits most websites; applications with background jobs or heavy traffic often need a VPS.

Do you set up business email?

Yes, including the DNS records that help your email avoid spam folders.
