Custom Web Application Development (Laravel & Python)

Custom web application development with Laravel and Python/Flask in Saudi Arabia: business platforms, portals and dashboards with roles, audit trails and reports.

When spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups and off-the-shelf tools stop keeping up with your operations, a custom web application brings the work into one system built around your process. I design and develop business platforms, portals and dashboards with Laravel and Python/Flask.

This is the work behind DMS and DOBS, two production systems I designed and built: more than 180 data models, role-based access, audit logging, payroll calculations, PDF documents and integrations with mobile apps. I bring the same structure to your project, scaled to what you need.

Laravel or Python for my project?

Both are excellent; I choose based on your team, existing systems and integrations, and explain the reasons.

Can you start with a smaller first version?

Yes. Starting with the most important workflow and expanding in phases is usually the best approach.

Who owns the code?

You do. You receive the source code, documentation and access to everything needed to run the system.
