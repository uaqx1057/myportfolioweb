Custom CMS Development

Custom CMS development with WordPress or Laravel: content models, editor workflows, user roles and multilingual Arabic/English content for your team.

A content management system should match how your team works, not force you into workarounds. I build content management around your real content: custom post types and admin screens in WordPress, or a lightweight Laravel-based CMS when off-the-shelf tools do not fit.

The result is a back office where editors find what they need, permissions are clear, Arabic and English content stay in sync, and publishing does not require a developer.

CMS Development: frequently asked questions

WordPress or a custom Laravel CMS?

WordPress is faster and cheaper for most websites; a custom Laravel CMS makes sense for complex workflows or when content is tied to business data.

Can different staff have different permissions?

Yes. Roles control who can create, edit, approve or publish each type of content.

Can you move content from my current site?

Yes. Content can be migrated with its structure, images and, where possible, existing URLs.
