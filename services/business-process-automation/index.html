Business Process Automation

Business process automation: scheduled reports, data imports, reconciliation, notifications, approvals and automatic documents that save hours of work.

Repetitive manual work, such as copying data between files, building the same report every week or chasing approvals by message, costs hours and causes errors. I automate those tasks with reliable scripts and scheduled jobs that run on their own and tell you when something needs attention.

In the DMS platform I built, around 50 scheduled and console jobs handle tasks such as imports, payroll preparation, anomaly checks and notifications. The same approach can remove manual steps from your operations.

What Business Automation includes

  • Workflow review to find what to automate
  • Scheduled jobs, imports and reconciliations
  • Email, WhatsApp and push notifications
  • Automatic PDF, report and document generation

Who this service is for

  • Teams building the same reports every week
  • Operations importing and reconciling data from partners
  • Processes that depend on approvals by email or chat
  • Businesses that generate many documents or PDFs

How the work runs

  1. Review your workflow and pick the highest-value task
  2. Design the automation and its checks
  3. Build and run it alongside the manual process
  4. Switch over once results match
  5. Monitor and extend to the next task

Business Automation: frequently asked questions

Which tasks can be automated?

Anything rule-based and repeated: reports, imports, reconciliations, reminders, approvals and document creation.

Do I need a new system for this?

Not necessarily. Many automations work with your existing files, spreadsheets and tools.

What if the automation makes a mistake?

Each automation includes checks and logs, and runs alongside the manual process until the results are proven.
