IT Project Management & Delivery

IT project management for web and mobile projects in Saudi Arabia: scope, estimates, sprints, developer coordination, updates and on-time releases.

Software projects rarely fail because of code alone. They fail on unclear scope, missed hand-offs and surprises late in the schedule. I manage web and mobile projects from planning to release: defining scope, estimating, coordinating developers and stakeholders, and keeping delivery predictable.

I have done this as Project Manager & Full-Stack Developer at iLab Information Technologies / SpeedLogi and as Project Manager & Senior WordPress Developer at The MK Solution, where I managed a 10-person cross-functional team. Because I am also a developer, technical risks are spotted early.

What Project Management & Delivery includes

  • Sprint planning and progress tracking
  • Stakeholder updates

Who this service is for

  • Companies outsourcing development to several vendors
  • Internal teams that need structure and regular releases
  • Founders who need a technical lead to manage the build

  4. Coordinate testing and releases
