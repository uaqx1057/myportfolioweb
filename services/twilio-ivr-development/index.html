Twilio IVR Systems & Voice Automation

Twilio IVR system development: phone menus, call routing, queues, voicemail, callbacks and call logging connected to your CRM or business system.

An interactive voice response (IVR) system greets callers, offers a menu and sends each call to the right person or department, so customers stop waiting and staff stop transferring calls by hand. I build IVR systems on Twilio with call routing, queues, after-hours messages, voicemail and callbacks.

Because Twilio is fully programmable, the phone system can talk to your business data: recognise returning customers, read out order or booking status, and log every call in your CRM or internal system.

Twilio IVR Systems & Voice: frequently asked questions

Can I keep my current phone number?

Often yes, through number porting or call forwarding; it depends on your provider and country rules.

Can callers choose Arabic or English?

Yes. The first menu can offer a language choice, with recordings in both languages.

How is Twilio billed?

Twilio charges for numbers and per minute of calls; I estimate the monthly cost from your call volume.
