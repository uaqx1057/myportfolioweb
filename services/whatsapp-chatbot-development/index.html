WhatsApp Chatbots for Business (Twilio)

WhatsApp chatbot development for businesses in Saudi Arabia using Twilio: automated replies, bookings, order updates and hand-off to staff, in Arabic and English.

Your customers already message you on WhatsApp. A WhatsApp chatbot answers the common questions instantly, day and night, takes bookings or orders, sends status updates and passes the conversation to a person when it needs a human. I build WhatsApp Business chatbots on Twilio, connected to your own system.

The bot can look up real data, such as order status, appointment times or account details, and every conversation can be logged so your team sees what customers ask most. Conversations are designed in Arabic and English.

  • Conversation design in Arabic and English
  • Twilio WhatsApp setup and message templates

Who this service is for

  • Shops and e-commerce stores answering order questions
  • Clinics, salons and services taking appointments
  • Logistics companies sending delivery updates

How the work runs

  1. List the questions and tasks the bot should handle
  2. Design the conversation flows in Arabic and English
  3. Set up Twilio, the WhatsApp sender and message templates
  4. Connect the bot to your system or database
  5. Test, launch and review the conversation logs

WhatsApp Chatbots for Business (Twilio): frequently asked questions

Do I need a WhatsApp Business account?

Yes. The bot uses an approved WhatsApp Business sender through Twilio, and I guide you through the registration steps.

Can a person take over the chat?

Yes. The bot can hand the conversation to your team when a request needs a human.

What does it cost to run?

Twilio and WhatsApp charge per conversation or message; I estimate running costs for your expected volume before we start.
