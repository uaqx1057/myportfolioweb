Website Maintenance & Support

Website maintenance and WordPress support: updates, backups, security hardening, monitoring, quick fixes and small content changes for live sites and stores.

A website is not finished at launch. Plugins and themes need updates, backups need testing, security needs watching, and small changes come up every month. I look after live websites and stores so they stay fast, secure and working, and you have one person to call when something needs fixing.

I have maintained multiple live client websites, handling updates, troubleshooting and improvements, and I bring the same discipline to every site: change carefully, back up first and keep a record.

Website Maintenance & Support: frequently asked questions

Can you maintain a site you did not build?

Yes. I start with a health check of the existing site and fix any risks I find.

Is maintenance monthly or on demand?

Both are possible: a regular monthly plan, or help when you need it.

What if my site gets hacked?

I clean the site, restore from a safe backup where possible, close the weakness and harden security.
