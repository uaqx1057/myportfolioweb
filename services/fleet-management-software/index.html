Fleet & Driver Management Software

Fleet management and driver management software for logistics companies in Saudi Arabia: onboarding, fleet, maintenance, attendance, payroll and driver apps.

Delivery and logistics companies manage drivers, vehicles, shifts, platforms and performance-based pay, usually across spreadsheets and messaging groups. I build fleet and driver management software that brings all of it into one system, with a mobile app for drivers.

This is not theory: I designed and built DMS, a driver and fleet management platform with a Flutter driver app used in daily last-mile operations in Saudi Arabia, and DOBS, the onboarding and offboarding system that feeds it. I can build or adapt the same capabilities for your operation.

What Logistics & Fleet Management Software includes

  • Driver onboarding and offboarding
  • Fleet, inspections and maintenance
  • Attendance, shifts and payroll
  • Driver mobile apps and dashboards

Who this service is for

  • Last-mile delivery companies working with several platforms
  • Fleet operators managing vehicles, inspections and maintenance
  • Companies paying drivers by targets, orders or performance
  • Operations that need a driver app with check-in and location

How the work runs

  1. Map your operation: drivers, vehicles, platforms and pay rules
  2. Prioritise modules: onboarding, fleet, attendance, payroll
  3. Build the web platform and driver app in phases
  4. Import existing data and run alongside current tools
  5. Train supervisors and drivers, then support

Logistics & Fleet Management Software: frequently asked questions

Can the system calculate driver pay automatically?

Yes. Pay rules, targets, tiers and deductions can be configured per platform, with salary slips and an audit trail.

Does it include a mobile app for drivers?

Yes. Drivers can check in, share location, see payroll and send requests from an Android app.

Can it be adapted to my company?

Yes. Modules, roles and rules are configured around your operation rather than forcing a fixed process.
